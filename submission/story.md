# Why I built Pulse

In the two days before this hackathon I lost a little over $25 trading Solana memecoins I found on pump.fun and DEX screeners. It isn't a big number, but it taught me exactly what was missing.

Three things went wrong:

1. **I was in profit and couldn't get out.** The chart said I was up. When I tried to sell, the sell returned far less than the chart promised, and by the time I tried again the profit had turned into a loss. I never knew how much I could actually sell for until it was too late.
2. **I aped fresh launches.** Coins minutes old, bought on momentum, with no idea who created them or who held the supply.
3. **I bought a copycat.** Same ticker as a coin people were talking about, different token.

Every one of those answers was already on Solana. It just took too many tabs and too much time to look, and memecoins don't wait.

So Pulse puts it on one screen in seconds:

- The **Exit Ladder** asks Jupiter, before you buy, what selling $100, $1k and $10k would actually return right now. That's the number I never had.
- **Holder concentration** and **creator history** show whether a few wallets hold the supply, or whether the creator has launched dozens of tokens before.
- **Copycat protection**: searches rank the verified, deepest-liquidity token first, and identity flags show when a ticker isn't the real one.
- Every verdict can be **anchored on Solana**, so what Pulse said, and when, can't be quietly changed afterwards.

Pulse doesn't tell anyone to buy or sell. It shows what I wish I'd seen before I signed.
